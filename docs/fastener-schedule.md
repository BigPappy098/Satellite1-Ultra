# Fastener schedule

`VERIFIED_DIGITALLY` for stack, engagement and bottoming margin. Torque is `ENGINEERING_ESTIMATE`.

Every insert bore is blind and is drilled deeper than the insert itself, so no screw can bottom on the bore floor. No fastener crosses the acoustic pressure boundary.
