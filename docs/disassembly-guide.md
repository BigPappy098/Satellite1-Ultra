# Disassembly guide

`VERIFIED_DIGITALLY`. Reverse of the validated assembly order; every part leaves along the reverse of its insertion direction.

1. reverse: stretch the TPU anti-slip ring onto the base
2. reverse: seat the official mid-plate and upper stack on the divider bosses
3. reverse: invert and bolt the outer shell to the bottom service plate
4. reverse: connect the boards and bolt the electronics shroud to the divider
5. reverse: seat the ballast cartridge and close the bottom service plate
6. reverse: lower the outer shell over the cabinet
7. reverse: bolt the base skirt up into the acoustic floor
8. reverse: fit the divider gasket and bolt the pressure divider to the cabinet
9. reverse: route the speaker pair through the divider and fit the TPU gland
10. reverse: fit both radiator gaskets and radiators, install both clamp rings
11. reverse: fit the active-driver gasket and driver, install the clamp ring
12. reverse: load the steel ballast, close the cartridge lid
13. reverse: install all heat-set inserts

## Rules

- Replace any gasket whose joint you open. Compressed closed-cell EPDM does not recover, and a reused seal is the most likely source of a leak.
- Never lever a clamp ring; it is a slip fit in its seat with 0.30 mm of radial clearance and will come out by hand once its four screws are out.
- Support the cabinet when the base skirt is off; the ballast is loose in its cartridge.
